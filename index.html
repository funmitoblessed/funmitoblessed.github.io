Olúwafúnmitọ́ ODEFEMI

Olúwafúnmitọ́ is Yorùbá for “God has given me to nurture.” I see this as more than a name; it reflects how I approach life. Through my work, relationships, and the knowledge I share, I seek to nurture growth through care and commitment.

I currently lead monitoring, evaluation and learning at Notify Health, making sure caregiver data travels accurately from the field into the systems that send vaccination reminders. Before that, I spent four and a half years building data quality systems at One Acre Fund Nigeria.

About

My career began at the physics department of the University of Ibadan, tutoring first-year students. Shortly afterwards, I volunteered with VSO Nigeria in Kwali, FCT, led teams of volunteers, and then kept the organization’s computers, networks and data running.

Each move since then has taken me deeper into data. At WAGARC and TGI Group I wrote monitoring plans, built KoboToolbox surveys and reported on programs. At One Acre Fund I programmed surveys in CommCare, built real-time data quality checks and automated slow processes, growing from coordinator to specialist. Today I lead MEL at Notify Health.

Teaching has stayed part of the work throughout. I’ve trained lead farmers, partner staff and colleagues in digital tools, and led weekly sessions for Udacity’s Data Analyst Nanodegree students.
